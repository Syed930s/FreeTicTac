💙 This game is charityware

FreeTicTac is free to play and free to use. There is no price, no ads, and no paywall. Instead, if you enjoy the game, we ask one simple thing: consider making a small donation to charity.

What is charityware?

Charityware is software that asks users to donate to a good cause instead of paying the author. You never have to pay to use it — but if it made you smile, passing some kindness forward is the whole point.

Where does the money go?

  • Donations go directly to UNICEF through their official donation page.
  • UNICEF works to protect children's rights, health, education, and safety around the world.
  • This website does not collect, handle, or see any money or payment details.
